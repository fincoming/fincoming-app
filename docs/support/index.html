Contact

For questions, feedback, or bug reports, email fincoming.admin@gmail.com. We typically respond within a few business days.

When reporting a bug, include your iOS version, device model, and what you were doing when the issue occurred. Screenshots are helpful. We cannot access your plan data — it stays on your device.

Common questions

Is FIncoming financial advice?

No. FIncoming is an educational scenario-planning tool. Projections use simplified models. Consult a licensed professional before making financial decisions. See our Terms of Service.

Where is my data stored?

All plan inputs are stored locally on your iPhone. We do not operate user accounts or cloud sync for plan data. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I reset my plan?

In the app, open the menu (⋯) on the dashboard and choose Start over from the beginning. That permanently deletes your saved plan on this device. Uninstalling the app also removes local data.

How do I edit my baseline inputs?

Open the dashboard menu and choose Edit my info to walk through the setup wizard again with your current values pre-filled.

Subscriptions (FIncoming Pro)

If you subscribe to FIncoming Pro, manage or cancel through Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Refunds are handled by Apple per their policies.
