Scenario planning for FIRE and near-retirees

FIncoming models taxes, Social Security, RMDs, Roth conversions, and spending year-by-year so you can compare a baseline plan against “what if” changes — without spreadsheets.

Not financial advice. FIncoming is an educational planning tool. Projections use simplified models. Consult a licensed professional before making financial decisions.

Your data stays on your device

Plan inputs are stored locally on your iPhone with SwiftData. We do not require an account, and we do not receive your financial figures on our servers. See the Privacy Policy for details.
