FIncoming ("the App") is a personal retirement scenario planner developed by Adam Oostema. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

1. Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any identifying information.

All plan inputs you enter — ages, income, account balances, spending targets, and similar figures — are stored exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or share:

  • Name, email address, or contact information
  • Financial account numbers or linked account data
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to third parties
  • Behavioral or tracking data of any kind

3. Third-Party Services

Current release: Tax tables, Social Security parameters, and other reference data are bundled inside the App. We do not currently integrate advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or data brokers.

We may add optional remote configuration in a future release (for example, to update tax brackets between App Store releases). If we do, that service would receive only non-personal configuration payloads — never your plan inputs. We will update this policy before any such service ships.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through the App, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

5. Data Security

Because all plan data stays on your device, its security is governed by your device's own protections — passcode, Face ID / Touch ID, and iOS device encryption. We do not have access to your data and cannot recover it if your device is lost or reset.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your plan data persists until you delete it within the App ("Start over from the beginning") or uninstall the App. Either action permanently removes all locally stored data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be noted with a revised "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
